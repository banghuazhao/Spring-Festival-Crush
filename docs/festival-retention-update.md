# Festival retention and game-feel update (3.3)

## Implemented

- **Continue offer.** Running out of moves (or time) now pauses on a "Keep going?" panel before the defeat screen, with a progress ring ("92% there") and the goals still missing. A continue adds 5 moves and/or 20 seconds, whichever ran out. It costs 30, 60, then 90 coins; the first continue of each attempt can instead be paid with a rewarded video. There are at most 3 per attempt. Giving up (or leaving via pause) still costs the life. If the guardian's raid was due on the move that ran out, it still happens after a continue, so buying moves never skips a raid. Pricing lives in `ContinueOffer`; the flow is in `GameModel.handleGameLose` / `acceptContinue…` / `declineContinue`.
- **Review prompt.** `ReviewPromptPolicy` asks for an App Store rating only after a 3-star clear or a defeated guardian, never after a failure. It also requires at least 6 lifetime wins, asks at most once per app version and waits 60 days between asks. StoreKit's own yearly cap still applies. The request is made 1.2 s after the victory stars land, and never from inside unit tests.
- **New tool: Ruyi Swap (如意换位).** Swaps any two neighbouring movable tiles without needing a match and without spending a move or advancing the boss. Swapping a power-up still fires it. Tiles cross on opposite gold/red arcs with a zither sweep. New players start with 2 charges. More come from a rewarded ad (tray "+"), the pre-level booster sheet (25 coins), daily envelopes and star chests. Arming it disarms the Hammer and vice versa. Icon: `SwapBoosterIcon`, drawn by `tools/draw_swap_booster_icon.py`.
- **Daily red envelope (每日红包) and a 7-day streak.** Rewards: 20 coins → Shuffle → 40 coins → Hammer → 60 coins → Ruyi Swap → day 7 (120 coins, Hammer, Swap, 2 lives), then the cycle repeats. Missing a whole calendar day restarts at day 1, and moving the clock backwards never unlocks an envelope. A rewarded video can double one claim. The envelope opens itself once per launch after the consent flow, and there is a toolbar button with a ready dot on the map. Rules: `DailyEnvelopeRules`; storage: `DailyEnvelopeModel`.
- **Star chests.** Each chapter has three chests at 1, 2 and 3 stars per level (15/30/45 for 15 levels). They sit on a progress rail under the chapter header. A ready chest bounces; opening it pops the lid with chime and haptics. Claims are stored per zodiac and refused twice (`StarChestStore`).
- **Audio.** 14 new sound effects, synthesized procedurally (no samples, no licensing): 8 guzheng strings on the D-major pentatonic scale, a zither glissando, gong, tanggu drum, firecracker string, firework and chime. Each cascade climbs one string. The finale plays one rising note per converted move. Lightning crackles like firecrackers, guardians hit with the drum and raid with the gong, and levels open with a glissando (or a gong for boss levels). Regenerate with `python3 tools/synthesize_festival_sfx.py SpringFestivalCrush/Sounds`.
- **Haptics.** Core Haptics patterns (`FestivalHaptics`) for explosions, the firecracker crackle, cascade depth, guardian hits scaled by damage, the raid's building rumble, reward openings, fireworks, and a soft double knock on a near miss. Devices without a Taptic Engine fall back to the previous UIKit generators. The existing haptics toggle still mutes everything.
- **Hit-stop.** The board freezes for 45–110 ms on enhanced blasts, the Five release, power-up combinations and big guardian hits, then snaps forward. Only the board layer stops, so sounds and the model's async flow keep going. Removals get back the frames they lost while frozen. Hit-stop is skipped with reduced motion, and restart/results cancel any pending freeze.
- **Festival Finale.** Every win opens its bonus with a "FESTIVAL FINALE!" banner, "N moves → fireworks!", and a volley of 2–7 fireworks (rockets, bursts, drooping embers). Each saved move plays a rising pluck as it becomes an enhanced tile. With reduced motion, only the banner and a chime are shown.
- **Guardian personality.** The HUD avatar bobs while idle. On a hit it flinches red, shows a "-N" damage pop and a trailing "ghost" health bar. One move before a raid it glares (💢, orange frame); on the raid it lunges with the gong, screen shake and a board-wide tint flash. It says short taunt lines per guardian. When defeated it keels over with 💫.
- **Localization.** Simplified and Traditional Chinese now cover every gameplay, map, results, settings and new-feature string, plus level hints, chapter names and guardian text (≈284 new keys per language). Shared containers (`GamePopupPanel`, notices, HUD banners, settings cards, booster rows, ad buttons) localize the text they display. `ChineseZodiac.name` stays English because it names the level files; `localizedName` is for display. Traditional Chinese is converted with OpenCC (`s2twp`) and uses 「」 quotes.
